## Codebase Patterns

- **Bundler architecture**: processNode → processObject → processRef for external refs. After fetching a remote schema, flattenDefs() extracts nested $defs/definitions and adds them to bctx.defs with prefixed keys.
- **Def flattening**: Nested definitions are flattened using `__` separator (e.g., `remote__Address`). Both internal refs and external fragment refs are rewritten to the flattened location.
- **Ref rewriting flow**: flattenDefs → rewriteNestedRefs (for flattened defs) + parseDefFragment (for external fragments pointing to defs)
