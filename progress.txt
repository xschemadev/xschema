## Codebase Patterns

- **Bundler architecture**: processNode → processObject → processRef for external refs. After fetching a remote schema, flattenDefs() extracts nested $defs/definitions and adds them to bctx.defs with prefixed keys.
- **Def flattening**: Nested definitions are flattened using `__` separator (e.g., `remote__Address`). Both internal refs and external fragment refs are rewritten to the flattened location.
- **Ref rewriting flow**: flattenDefs → rewriteNestedRefs (for flattened defs) + parseDefFragment (for external fragments pointing to defs)

## 2026-01-12 - US-002 & US-004
- Implemented nested definition flattening and fragment ref rewriting
- Files changed: cli/bundler/bundler.go, cli/bundler/bundler_test.go
- **What was implemented:**
  - `flattenDefs()` - extracts $defs/definitions from embedded schemas, prefixes keys with parent key using `__`
  - `rewriteNestedRefs()` - rewrites refs in flattened defs to point to new locations
  - `parseDefFragment()` - detects `/definitions/X` or `/$defs/X` fragments and extracts the def name
  - Fragment refs like `foo.json#/definitions/Bar` now rewritten to `#/$defs/foo_key__Bar`
- **Learnings:**
  - US-002 and US-004 are tightly coupled - can't flatten defs without also fixing fragment refs
  - Tests needed updating to expect flattened structure instead of nested paths
  - Recursive flattening handles defs with their own nested defs
